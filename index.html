04

Solar-Powered EV Charger

Engineered a solar-powered Tesla Level 2 EV charger in a three-person team — integrating eight 240W solar panels, a 48V battery system, an AC/DC inverter, and a 2500W charge controller delivering 2.4 kWh of energy. Coordinated local sponsor outreach resulting in a $4,000 technical supply donation. Featured by Fort Bend ISD and on Instagram.

Project 04 — Level 2 Solar EV Charger

System Overview

Designed and built a grid-independent solar charging station capable of delivering Level 2 EV charging power. The system manages MPPT solar input, battery storage, AC inversion, and J1772-compatible charge delivery — all from eight rooftop solar panels.
